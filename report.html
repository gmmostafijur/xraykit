FAQ

About the report — and everything after it

What a report contains, how the numbers work, and how posting and hashtag research fit in.

What exactly is in a report?

An overall 0–100 score, five frame scores (bio, pinned post, cadence, content mix, launch angle), the problems ranked by impact, what's already working, written fixes with ready-to-post drafts, and a 7-day plan — like the sample report on this site.

How is the score calculated?

Each of the five frames is scored 0–100 against what a first-time visitor needs to see; the overall score is their average, weighted toward the bio and pinned post because that's where profile visits are won or lost.

Is the “after” score a promise?

No — it's a clearly labelled estimate of what the same scan would find if every fix shipped. Pro lets you re-scan after the fixes, which replaces the estimate with your real new score.

Do you post to X for me?

At launch, posting is manual: XRayKit gives you finished drafts, a dated plan and copy buttons, and you post on X or use X's own free scheduler. That keeps it free of X API credit costs. Auto-posting is planned as a later, higher tier.

How does hashtag research work?

Give it a topic and it suggests hashtags with daily posts, daily comments, total volume and a competition rating — high, medium or low — so you can mix one big tag with quieter ones your post can actually rank in.

How does the 3-day trial work?

You get full Pro from the moment you start — no card required. When day 3 ends, the trial stops on its own; if you want to keep Pro, add a card then and it continues at $7/month.
