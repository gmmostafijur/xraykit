    Questions

    Why do I paste my posts instead of just giving my handle?

    Reading X data automatically needs X's paid API, which would force the price far above $7/month. Pasting takes 30 seconds, needs no account connection, and keeps XRayKit cheap. Automatic fetching is on the roadmap once revenue covers the API cost.

    Is this only for crypto founders?

    The audit checks — hooks, proof, positioning, call to action — are tuned for crypto and Web3 accounts, where trust is the whole game. The same report format works for any founder building in public.

    What happens when my 3-day trial ends?

    Nothing is charged — no card was taken. Your account returns to the Free plan (1 audit/month, top 2 problems). Upgrade to Pro at $7/month whenever you're ready.

    Will the $7 price change for me later?

    $7 is the launch price and it will go up for new subscribers as XRayKit grows. Starting at launch pricing is how you lock in the lowest rate.

    Can I audit a competitor's account?

    Yes — Pro includes 30 audits per month. Paste any public profile's bio and recent posts and you'll get the same breakdown, so you can see exactly what the accounts winning your niche are doing differently.
